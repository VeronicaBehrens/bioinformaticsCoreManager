The Basics of Jupyter Notebooks¶

1. Jupyter Notebooks are made up of cells. You run each cell of code separately.

  To run the code in a cell:
    a. Click anywhere within the cell
    b. Hold down "shift" and hit "enter"

In [ ]:
# Run this cell!
print('hello')

2. To rename your notebook, click on the current title and type a new name.


3. To save your notebook, hit the "Floppy disk"

4. To add a cell to your notebook, hit the "Plus"

5. To remove a cell from your notebook, hit the "Scissors"

6 To move a cell up or down, use the "Arrows"

In [ ]:
# Add two cells below.
# Delete one of the them.
# In the remaining cell, print out the phrase "hi there"

7. To interrupt a cell while it's executing: Hit the "Square"


8. To clear the output of all cells in the entire notebook: "Kernel" --> "Restart & Clear Output"

9. To run all cells in the entire notebook: "Kernel" --> "Restart & Run All" or "Cell" --> "Run All"

10. To see all the keyboard shortcuts: "Help" --> "Keyboard Shortcuts"¶

Helpful features within Jupyter Notebooks¶

How to get help:

  1. Reference the official documentation: https://docs.python.org/3.7/contents.html
    For example, the "Data Structures" chapter (https://docs.python.org/3.7/tutorial/datastructures.html) may be helpful for material in the first week!

  2. Use Jupyter Notebooks to your advantage!
    a. Use help() or "shift" "tab" to view documentation
    b. See all the available methods by hitting "tab" after a .
    c. Use "tab" to autocomplete
